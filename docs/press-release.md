# Press release - SteerCo

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## SteerCo brings investment clarity to product leadership - without another planning silo

**London, UK** - Today, Matthew Worthington introduces **SteerCo**, a steering workspace for product owners, product executives, and the Engineering Directors who partner with them - leaders who need a clear answer to three questions: _Are we funding the right goals? Is the organisation shaped for fast flow of value? What should we stop?_

Most enterprises already have Jira for work, Backstage for services, and Entra or GitHub for people and teams. None of those tools own the **investment contract** - the living link between business goals, Measures of Success, funded bets, team topology intent, and the evidence that should trigger a stop or re-scope. Leaders end up stitching slide decks by hand. Alignment decays between steering committees.

SteerCo treats that contract as a first-class artifact, drawing on value-driven portfolio practice (**EDGE**: Lean Value Tree plus product mindset, Tech@Core, Periodic Value Review, Integrated Backlogs, Measures of Success, and six operating principles), **Domain-Driven Design** (Eric Evans: bounded contexts and ubiquitous language for problem-space boundaries), and modern team-of-teams design (Team Topologies). Leaders open a local workspace, shape a plain-language map of goals and bets, organise how work flows across customer-facing teams and shared platforms along clear domain boundaries, and leave with a **decision note** fit for a board pack - not a backlog.

### Not another system of record

SteerCo does not replace planning tools or directories. When organisations later connect Backstage, GitHub, or Entra, SteerCo **references** teams that already exist. It never invents competing catalog Groups for provider-synced estates. Technical exports and overlays remain optional; the default experience stays executive-safe.

### Built beside ArchLens - deliberately different

SteerCo sits in the same product family as **ArchLens**, the living systems-architecture suite. The relationship is intentional and narrow: a funded bet may later point at architecture risk in ArchLens. The experiences do **not** share a visual language. ArchLens is a builder’s tool. SteerCo is a steering-committee surface - light, typographic, and free of YAML in the default views.

### Local-first by design

The first release works entirely on the user’s machine: no account, no cloud sync required. Leaders can trial the model on a real transformation narrative before connecting any system. Authentication and live integrations follow once the SteerSpec contract proves useful in the room where decisions are made.

### Availability

Product specification, mockups, and architecture are published in draft under the author’s open portfolio. A dedicated open-source repository and Slice 1 implementation will follow.
